01 / The architecture

The seal is the system.

Three design decisions hold the shape: supply is fixed, the issuance curve is global, and the market is allowed to discover itself after the opening gate.

Ⅰ

One body

21,000,000 PHN is the complete genesis supply. Public issuance, protocol liquidity, founder, rewards and launch enablement are explicit allocations.

Ⅱ

One ascent

The curve moves with cumulative public acquisition and never resets at an epoch boundary. Its terminal $0.12 is an issuance boundary, not a market promise.

Ⅲ

One opening

Transfers are locked only through KHAOS's first 48 hours. At the exact boundary they become fully and permanently available; later epochs transfer immediately.
