What is PHANES. An immutable fixed-supply emission on Base. Time opens each stage. The curve does not reset. There is no owner, upgrade, or pause path on the intended production design.

Transferability. The first 48 hours of KHAOS protect the opening issuance envelope. At 23 December 2026 · 20:50 UTC, PHN transfer, approval and secondary-market compatibility become fully available and never relock. Later epochs continue primary issuance independently. Protocol-owned liquidity is separate and is not automatically deployed at this boundary.

Status. Experimental software. Not audited. RC10 v0.7 is an off-mainnet candidate; static/model validation is complete and fresh Solidity/Forge EVM reproduction remains required before testnet. Historical RC7 / RC9 deployments are unsupported and are not the launch path. The public Buy UI remains withheld until one canonical production deployment is verified and armed.
